🛡️ SECURITY+ PHYSICAL SECURITY FLASHCARDS

Mantrap (Access Portal): A specialized small room with two interlocking doors where the first door must close and lock before the second door opens. Prevents tailgating (unauthorized person following an authorized person) and piggybacking.
Hot & Cold Aisles: Server racks positioned so cool air intakes face each other (cold aisle) and hot exhaust air vents out the back (hot aisle), maximizing HVAC cooling efficiency and preventing thermal shutdowns.
Clean Agent Suppression: Chemical/inert gas extinguishing systems (Inergen, FM-200) that displace oxygen to snuff electrical fires without water spray.
