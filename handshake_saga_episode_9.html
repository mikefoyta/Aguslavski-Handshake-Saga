🛡️ POST-QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY (PQC) FLASHCARDS

Lattice-Based Cryptography (ML-KEM / CRYSTALS-Kyber): Standardized by NIST. Relies on the hardness of high-dimensional geometric lattice problems (Learning With Errors) that even quantum computers cannot solve efficiently.
Stateful Hash-Based Signatures (SLH-DSA / SPHINCS+): Quantum-resistant digital signatures based on hash tree mathematics rather than discrete logarithms.
Quantum Key Distribution (QKD): Uses photon polarization states to exchange cryptographic keys. The Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle guarantees that measurement by a third party alters the photon, revealing the spy.
