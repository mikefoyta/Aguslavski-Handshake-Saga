📖 VISUAL ENCYCLOPEDIA NODE // AI & HPC NETWORKING

RDMA (Remote Direct Memory Access): Allows network adapters (NICs) to transfer data directly into GPU/CPU memory across machines without involving the host OS or CPU interrupts, slashing latency from milliseconds to sub-microseconds.
InfiniBand vs RoCE: High-bandwidth, lossless fabric tailored for AI clusters (InfiniBand native, or RoCE: RDMA over Converged Ethernet).
GPU Starvation: When network bandwidth or latency bottlenecks prevent training batches from reaching Tensor cores in time, wasting costly enterprise GPU compute cycles.

🛡️ AI & CYBER AUDITING FLASHCARDS

Black Box Problem: Deep neural networks contain millions of parameters and hidden layers, making it mathematically opaque to trace why a specific inference was made.
Explainable AI (XAI): Techniques such as SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) and Saliency Maps that reveal which specific input features influenced the AI's final prediction.
Overfitting & Bias: When a machine learning model memorizes spurious coincidences in training data (like font choices or irrelevant background pixels) rather than genuine underlying patterns.
