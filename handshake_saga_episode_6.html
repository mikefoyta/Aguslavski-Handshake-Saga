🛡️ SECURITY+ & CYSA+ EXAM SHOWDOWN // HONEYPOTS & APTs

Honeypot: A decoy computer system intended to mimic likely targets of cyberattacks. It contains no real data, so ANY interaction with it is by definition suspicious and alerts the SOC.
APT (Advanced Persistent Threat): A sophisticated attacker (often nation-state) who establishes long-term undetected presence inside a network, frequently 'living off the land' (using native OS binaries like PowerShell/WMI rather than noisy malware).
