🛡️ SECURITY+ WIRELESS ENCRYPTION STANDARDS

WPA2 vs WPA3: WPA2 was vulnerable to offline dictionary attacks via the 4-way handshake. WPA3 uses Simultaneous Authentication of Equals (SAE) to prevent offline cracking even with weak passwords.
Protected Management Frames (802.11w): Cryptographically protects Wi-Fi management frames, preventing attackers from launching Deauthentication (Deauth) disconnect attacks.
Wireless Intrusion Prevention System (WIPS): Continuously monitors radio frequencies for rogue APs and rogue SSIDs, automatically suppressing them.
